1. Open an inspection from the search page and read its findings.
2. On the finding that needs a second look, choose **Flag for follow-up**. A note field appears next to the finding.
3. Type a short note, for example "Confirm tire brand before sale", and save.
4. The finding now shows the follow-up with its note, and a clear "saved" confirmation appears.
5. Continue reading; flag another finding the same way.
6. Come back later, open the same inspection, find the follow-up and choose **Mark resolved** once it is dealt with.
7. The follow-up shows as resolved and stays visible with the note.

What this journey does **not** say (exactly the five gaps the product clarification record fills):

- what exactly gets saved (which record, which fields, what identifies it);
- what happens when the same finding is flagged twice;
- what counts as a valid note (empty, whitespace, very long);
- whether "come back later" survives a page reload or a server restart;
- what "resolved" means: whether it changes the finding, whether it can be undone, whether it can be done twice.
