> **Follow-ups on inspection findings**
>
> Let users flag anything concerning and come back to it later. Make it feel polished, with good design and sleek edges. Avoid clutter and make saving obvious.
>
> Context from the inspection desk lead: reviewers read an inspection, spot a finding that needs a second look (a tire brand to confirm, a dent to price, a photo to request) and today they write it on a sticky note. They want to leave a short note on the finding itself and clear it when it is dealt with. Nothing leaves the inspection desk; no notifications, no assignment to other people for now.
